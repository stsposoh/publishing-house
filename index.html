Turn the page

We publish slowly.

Sirius began in 1987 in a former bindery on Lantern Row, with one hand-fed press and a conviction that some books should take longer to make than to read. We still believe it.

New this season: The Cartographer of Small Rooms, by Ilse Varga

A retired mapmaker begins charting the apartment she can no longer leave — every crack in the plaster becomes a river, every draught a prevailing wind. A novel about the size of a life.

Every copy, made by hand.

Sewn bindings. Cotton papers from a mill in the Ardèche. Covers set in metal type. Small editions, numbered and signed. 39 years on Lantern Row, 64 titles in print, 11 languages.
